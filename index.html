Treatments

Every session starts with a chat about what your body is doing, then the pressure and style get matched to you. Sessions run 30, 60 or 90 minutes.

Unwind

Relaxation massage

Slow, flowing full-body work that settles the nervous system. The one to book when stress has moved in and will not leave.

Release

Deep tissue massage

Firm, focused pressure into the muscles that actually hold the tension. Expect honest work, not a light rub over the top.

Recover

Remedial massage

Targeted treatment for niggles, old injuries and overworked muscles. Good for gardeners, tradies and weekend sport casualties.

Warmth

Hot stone massage

Heated stones melt through tight muscle before hands-on work begins. Deep warmth without deep pressure.

Ground

Reflexology and foot massage

Pressure-point work through the feet that eases the whole body. A quiet favourite for people on their feet all day.

Restore

Lymphatic and pregnancy massage

Gentle lymphatic drainage and side-lying prenatal massage, adjusted around whatever stage you are at.

38 Childers Road

A quiet room in the middle of Gisborne

Body and Soul runs from the Poverty Bay Club on Childers Road, a short walk from the centre of town. One therapist, one treatment room, no rush between clients.

You get asked what is going on with your body before anything else. The treatment gets built around that answer, whether it is a sore neck from the desk, a back that seized up on the job, or a head that will not switch off.

  • Locally owned and operated in Gisborne
  • Pressure and style matched to you, every visit
  • Easy central location with parking close by
